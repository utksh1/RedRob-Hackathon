## 🥈 TRACK 2 (Ideathon) — PS-wise best ideas

### 📌 PS1 (Technical AI system design) — best ideas:

#### 💎 IDEA 1 ⭐⭐⭐ — "SkillProof" (AI Skill-Verification Engine)
> Redrob ka "Skill Tests" + "Skills Leaderboard" feature — par India-first.

**👤 Kahani:** Ek MNC ke paas 5000 resume aaye. Sab likhte hain "Expert in Python." Kaun sach mein expert hai? Aur ek Tier-3 student jo sach mein expert hai par college unknown hai — woh kaise prove kare?

**🎯 Idea:** Ek AI jo resume ke claims ko **live test** se verify kare — chhote, role-specific, AI-generated tasks de ke. Har candidate ka **"Verified Skill Score"** (degree/college se independent). Test apni bhasha mein de sako.

**🏆 Kyun jeetega:** Felix ki "visibility problem" ko directly solve karta hai (talent dikhne lagta hai, degree matter nahi karti). Plus Redrob ka coming-soon feature. Double hit.

**📊 Deck ke 10 slides:**
1. Title: "SkillProof — Degree nahi, skill bolti hai"
2. Problem: 5000 resume, sab "expert", kaun sach?
3. Kitna bada: India mein crore graduates, lakhs fake/inflated claims
4. Solution: AI live skill-test → Verified Skill Score
5. Kaise kaam karta: diagram (resume → AI test generate → score)
6. Multilingual: test apni bhasha mein
7. Visibility angle: Tier-3 talent ab prove kar sakta hai
8. User journey: candidate + recruiter dono ka view
9. Business: recruiters ko verified pool becho (B2B)
10. Vision: "Hiring ab naam pe nahi, hunar pe"

#### 💎 IDEA 2 ⭐⭐⭐ — "OutreachIQ" (Autonomous GTM/Sales Agent) — 🆕 NAYA OUTSTANDING IDEA
> **Yeh sabse smart move hai.** Redrob ka aadha business **sales/GTM** hai (People Search, 700M profiles, "Redrob GTM"). 95% participants sirf hiring sochenge — tum sales socho ge → judge turant samajh jaayega ki tum Redrob ko **deeply** jaante ho.

**👤 Kahani:** Ek startup ka sales banda roz 100 prospects dhoondhta hai, har ek ko manually research karta hai, email likhta hai — 80% time waste, kam conversions.

**🎯 Idea:** Ek autonomous AI agent jo: sahi prospects dhoondhe (intent signals se) → unki research kare → personalized outreach likhe (sahi waqt pe) → reply handle kare → meeting book kare. "AI sales rep."

**⚙️ Kaise:** Intent-detection agent → research agent → personalization agent → timing/send agent → reply agent. Human approval optional.

**🏆 Kyun jeetega:** Redrob GTM ka exact roadmap. "Most AI gives answers, Redrob gets work done" — yeh literally kaam (sales) karta hai. Aur kyunki kam log GTM sochenge, tum bheed se alag.

#### 💎 IDEA 3 ⭐⭐ — "HireFlow" (Chhote business ka hiring copilot)
> Redrob ke hiring + multilingual ka mel.

**👤 Kahani:** Ek small-business owner 12-logon ki company chalata hai. Accountant chahiye — na HR, na hiring budget, na JD likhna aata.

**🎯 Idea:** WhatsApp pe AI hiring copilot. Owner apni bhasha mein bole *"Tally-wala accountant, 2 saal"* → AI JD banaye → candidates shortlist kare → screen kare → interview fix kare. Sab vernacular, WhatsApp pe.

**🏆 Kyun jeetega:** 6 crore+ MSMEs ka real pain + WhatsApp + Hindi = pure India. (Dhyaan: candidate-finding Redrob ke People Search jaisa — toh **agentic automation + vernacular** pe zor do.)
