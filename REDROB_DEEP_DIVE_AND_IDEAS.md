#### 💎 IDEA 1 ⭐⭐⭐ — "SkillProof" (AI Skill-Verification Engine)

**Objective:** Build an AI that verifies the skill claims on a resume using a live, AI-generated test — so hiring is based on proof, not labels. Multilingual. Degree-independent. (Based on Redrob's "Skill Tests" Coming Soon feature.)

**👤 Story:**
A self-taught developer from a Tier-3 town has built real apps that people use. Their college is "unknown," so on their resume they simply write "Expert in Python." A recruiter has 5,000 resumes — every single one says "expert." This developer's real skill is invisible. They get rejected in the first filter. Meanwhile, someone who only *wrote* the right words but can't actually code gets through. The system rewards the claim, not the skill.

**🎯 What to build (simple words):**
An AI tool that reads a resume, instantly generates a short, role-specific practical test for each claimed skill, lets the candidate take it (in their own language), and produces a **"Verified Skill Score" (0–100)** per skill. Recruiters then see candidates ranked by *proven* skill — not by college name.

**⚙️ How it works (step by step):**
1. **Claim Extractor (LLM):** reads the resume and pulls out the skills + the claimed level (e.g., "Python — expert, SQL — intermediate").
2. **Test Generator (LLM):** for each skill, generates 2–3 short practical micro-tasks (a coding skill → a small code task; data analysis → a dataset question; communication → a written scenario).
3. **Candidate takes the test:** answers by text or voice, in their preferred language. Takes ~15 minutes.
4. **Evaluator (LLM + rubric):** grades each answer with partial credit, against a clear scoring rubric.
5. **Score Engine:** combines everything into a Verified Skill Score per skill.
6. **Explainer + Recruiter Dashboard:** recruiter sees verified candidates with a breakdown ("Python 88, verified by 3 tasks"), with college/name hidden to remove bias.

**🏆 Why it wins (why judges love it):**
- It directly solves Felix's #1 belief — the "visibility problem." A Tier-3, self-taught person can finally *prove* their skill without a brand-name degree.
- It's literally Redrob's "Skill Tests" Coming Soon feature — you're prototyping their roadmap.
- It's multilingual (their core strength) and execution-focused ("AI that works, not chats").
- It creates clear B2B revenue (recruiters pay for a verified talent pool). Felix is a business mind — he'll love this.

**📊 What to show in the deck/build (concrete):**
- Slide 2: the developer's story (before: invisible → after: verified & hired).
- A clean architecture diagram of the 5 components.
- Two mockups: the candidate's test screen + the recruiter's verified-dashboard.
- A "Verified Skill Score" sample card (e.g., "Python 88 · SQL 76 · verified via live tasks").
- A "Redrob fit" slide: their product list with "Skill Tests (Coming Soon)" highlighted → arrow to SkillProof.
- A tech slide: "GPT-4o reasoning · Judge0 code verification · IndicTrans2 for 22 languages · served cost-efficiently on open models."

#### 💎 IDEA 2 ⭐⭐⭐ — "OutreachIQ" (Autonomous Sales/GTM Agent) — 🆕 THE STANDOUT

**Objective:** Build an autonomous AI agent that runs the entire sales/outreach cycle on its own — find → research → write → send → reply → book a meeting. (Based on Redrob's sales/GTM business — People Search, 700M profiles, "Redrob GTM.")

**👤 Story:**
A salesperson at an early-stage startup starts every morning the same way: find 100 prospects manually, research each one on Google/LinkedIn, then write 100 different emails. The result: 6 hours of work, 80% of it spent on research, and a 2% reply rate. Burnout — and deals still slip away. Their real job (selling, talking to people) gets buried under admin.

**🎯 What to build (simple words):**
An AI "sales employee." You just give it a goal ("I want customers like X"). It then finds the right prospects, researches them, writes a personalized message for each, sends at the right time, handles replies, and books meetings — while you stay in control with optional approvals.

**⚙️ How it works (step by step):**
1. **You set a goal:** e.g., "SaaS founders, 10–50 employees."
2. **Finder Agent:** finds matching prospects using intent signals (the kind of data Redrob's 700M-profile People Search holds).
3. **Researcher Agent:** builds a short summary of each prospect (role, company, recent activity).
4. **Writer Agent:** drafts a personalized message in the right tone and language.
5. **Timing Agent:** picks the best time to send.
6. **Responder Agent:** reads replies, follows up, and books meetings on your calendar.
7. **Human approval gate (optional):** you review/approve before anything goes out. A "Manager Agent" orchestrates all of them.

**🏆 Why it wins:**
- This is Redrob's exact GTM roadmap — and **95% of teams will only think about hiring**, so thinking about sales instantly signals "this team understands Redrob's full business."
- It embodies their motto perfectly: *"AI doesn't just answer — it works."* This is an AI employee, not a chatbot.
- Clear B2B revenue: every sales team would pay for an "AI rep."
- Fewer teams attempt it → maximum "deep understanding" differentiation.

**📊 What to show in the deck/build:**
- Slide 2: the salesperson's exhausting day (clock + 12 browser tabs).
- A multi-agent architecture diagram (Manager + 5 agents + human-approval gate).
- A dashboard mockup: prospect list + draft messages + a "12 meetings booked" counter.
- A "before vs after" slide: "6 hours of research" → "30 minutes, 12 meetings booked."
- A "Redrob fit" slide: People Search / GTM data → arrow → "OutreachIQ (autonomous)."
